# Dataset
RUMI, ARHAM. 2021. Amazon Product Reviews. https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/arhamrumi/amazon-product-reviews.

# Introduction:
Dealing with big data is difficult, especially when it is done manually, and when it is related to emotions, it is too difficult. This is where NLP comes in, the NLP defines as a field combined between computer science, artificial intelligence and computational linguistics aim to understand and interpret human language in the way that resembles humans.(Jurafsky and H. Martin 2026).
 NLP analyzes emotions and turns them into something extremely easy. The dataset of Amazon customer reviews is one example of what models can be trained to read easily.

# Problem Definition:
The difficulty of classifying more than 565000 records of customer reviews manually on purpose to sentiment analysis.

# Motivation:
Now, with the development of e-commerce, Amazon receive a lot of daily reviews and ratings from customers about different products. For any company or vendor, especially a big company like Amazon, it is humanly impossible to manually read and rate all these reviews to understand the level of customer satisfaction. The motivation of this project is using NLP techniques to automate the process of analyzing emotions, that’s enabling companies to identify product weakness points for improving their quality and enhance their marketing strategies based on real and immediate customer feedback.

# Problem Statement:
The main problem is the stars’ rating is not enough to provide an explanation for customer satisfaction or pouts, also the raw text observations are unstructured data that is difficult to analyze on a large scale. The aim of this project is producing a strong classification system depend on NLP technique to classify amazon product reviews automatically to positive or negative feelings. This system will solve the challenges of customers’ language processing like understanding context and complex sentence structure to produce accurate evaluation reflect customer opinion that’s help managers to make data-driven decisions.

# Expected Result:
Trying to access a copy of revised version of Amazon reviews ready to analyse by using advanced text preprocessing techniques like Tokenization, Lemmatization, TF-IDF methods and Stemming. To obtain accurate classification models, we will effort to implement two models by using libraries like SKLEARN, NUMPY, Pandas and Keras to apply Naive Bayes and LSTM to compare their efficiency in predicting emotions. One of the most important expected Result is produce visualizations focuses on the most of influential words in customer satisfaction or complaints. The most visualizations are word clouds and frequency charts.
